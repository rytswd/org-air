*rule (B), NEUTRAL-ONLY*: =org-air-view--day-relative-face= was wired into

still reads in its slot colour — the user's "still in blue" — and never

R87 revises the ruling to *rule (A): TODAY / TOMORROW WINS*.  The one-line
change is to route the SAME =org-air-view--day-relative-face= into the

precisely because =--date-label= hands the deadline/scheduled SLOT face to
=--item-date-text=, which both propertizes the text AND passes that same
face to =--svg-pillify=.  Driving the day face into those two arms
therefore reaches BOTH the plain coloured text AND the svg pill in one
face-only move — no svg-specific code, no new pill colour parameter.

This stays a *face-only* change (R53): the label BYTES ("Today"/

* Decision (stated first — rule A supersedes R85's rule B)

*A delta-0 (today) / delta-1 (tomorrow) date takes the
=org-air-face-day-today= / =-day-tomorrow= face on EVERY date slot —
deadline, scheduled AND neutral.  R85's rule (B) NEUTRAL-ONLY is retired.*
An OVERDUE (past) deadline still keeps =org-air-face-overdue=; a date two
or more days out keeps its slot face exactly as before.

*Why (A) now, when R85 chose (B):*

1. *The user's ask is explicit and it is about the due date.*  R85
   reasoned that the slot COLOUR (orange=deadline, purple/blue=scheduled)
   carries meaning that matters *most* on a today-deadline, so it declined
   to repaint it.  The user has now told us the opposite: on the due-date
   column the slot colour "doesn't stand out too much" and they want the
   TODAY/TOMORROW standout to win *there specifically*.  When the user
   states the trade-off directly, least-surprise means honouring it —
   rule (A) is the change R85 explicitly flagged as "a one-line change
   (drop the neutral-arm gate)" for exactly this pivot.

2. *A today/tomorrow deadline is STILL legible as urgent — teal/rose are
   accent hues, not the muted grey.*  R85's fear was that recolouring
   would "destroy the this-is-a-deadline signal".  But (i) the row is
   *already* flagged as a deadline by its section/heading and by the label
   text ("Today"/"Tomorrow" on a dated row is unmistakably a due date),
   and (ii) the day faces are bold, saturated accents — the date does not
   go quiet, it goes *louder and differently coloured*, which is precisely
   "stand out more".  The semantic that R85 wanted to protect (this is
   time-critical) is reinforced, not lost: a bold teal "Today" on the due
   date reads MORE urgent than a slot-orange "Today", not less.

3. *OVERDUE is untouched — the genuinely critical case keeps its critical
   face.*  The two =overdue= arms sit BEFORE the deadline/scheduled arms
   in the cond and fire for PAST dates (=(> (days-between DL now) 0)=),
   emitting "OVERDUE Nd" in =org-air-face-overdue= (bold critical red).
   =--day-relative-face= only ever answers non-nil for delta ∈ {0,1}
   (today/tomorrow) and is consulted ONLY on the deadline/scheduled/notes
   arms, which a past date never reaches.  So delta<0 (overdue) is
   confirmed unchanged *by construction* — the standout is a
   TODAY/TOMORROW affordance, never an overdue one.

4. *Consistency across the three date arms.*  Under (B) the standout was
   asymmetric — a today NOTE lit up teal but a today DEADLINE did not,
   which is itself surprising (the more important date stood out less).
   Rule (A) makes the rule uniform and easy to state: *any* date that is
   today/tomorrow, on *any* slot, stands out in the day face; every other
   date keeps its slot face.  One rule, no per-slot exceptions.

*The cost of the pivot (accepted, and small):* the R85 rule-B tests that
pinned "a deadline/scheduled today/tomorrow KEEPS its slot face"
(round85 r85-5/6/11/12) and the two R3 date-label face assertions
(=org-air-date-future-{deadline,scheduled}-is-benign=) are now WRONG and
must be re-blessed to the rule-A expectation (they carry the day face).
This is a deliberate contract revision, not a regression — the OVERDUE
sign-inversion contract those R3 tests primarily guard (future ≠ OVERDUE)
is *unchanged*; only their secondary face assertion flips.  §ERT
enumerates every seam to replace.

The *composite* alternative (C) — keep the slot hue but ADD a
today/tomorrow emphasis (bold+underline), so a deadline-today reads
"deadline AND today" — is re-examined and re-rejected in §Alternatives:
it turns the =(LABEL . FACE)= FACE into a face LIST / anonymous merged
face, ripples into =--item-date-text= and =--svg-pillify= (which take ONE
face symbol and read ONE =face-foreground= for the pill =:fill=), and
still would not give the user the clean "the due date is now teal" they
asked for.  Rule (A) is the direct, contract-simple answer.

* Investigation (read the code)

** The call sites — the exact three arms, and which one R85 wired

=org-air-view--date-label= (org-air-view.el:2064) is the sole chooser of
the date cell's =(LABEL . FACE)=.  Its cond, in order:

#+begin_example
(cond
 ((and deadline  (> (days-between deadline now) 0))              ; OVERDUE (past DL)
  (cons "OVERDUE Nd" 'org-air-face-overdue))
 ((and scheduled (> (days-between scheduled now) 0))             ; OVERDUE (past SCHED)
  (cons "OVERDUE Nd" 'org-air-face-overdue))
 (deadline  (cons (--human-date deadline now)  'org-air-face-deadline))   ; ← ARM 3
 (scheduled (cons (--human-date scheduled now) 'org-air-face-scheduled))  ; ← ARM 4
 ((eq bucket 'attention) (cons "no date" 'org-air-face-date))
 ((eq bucket 'notes)                                             ; ← ARM 6 (R85 wired)
  (when-let* ((activity (org-air-item-activity item)))
    (cons (--human-date activity now)
          (or (--day-relative-face activity now) 'org-air-face-date))))
 ((eq bucket 'stale) (cons "· Nd quiet" 'org-air-face-date)))
```

- *Arm 3 (deadline)* and *Arm 4 (scheduled)* call =--human-date= (so they
  DO emit "Today"/"Tomorrow" for delta 0/1) but hard-code the SLOT face.
  These are the arms the user is looking at ("due date column") and the
  ones R85 left alone.  *These are the two call sites R87 rewires.*
- *Arm 6 (notes)* is the ONLY arm R85 wired to =--day-relative-face= —
  the neutral case, already correct and *unchanged* by R87.
- The two =overdue= arms (past dates), *attention* ("no date") and *stale*
  ("· Nd quiet") never emit a relative today/tomorrow and are untouched.

So R87 is a two-arm edit (deadline + scheduled), symmetric with what R85
already did to the notes arm — the same =(or (--day-relative-face … now)
'org-air-face-SLOT)= idiom, applied to the two remaining human-date arms.

** The svg crux — the pill's colour IS the face foreground (no separate path)

The user said "svg".  Tracing the board date cell:

=org-air-view--item-date-text= (org-air-view.el:3615) takes
=(LABEL . FACE)= from =--date-label= and builds the cell:

#+begin_example
(let* ((face (or (cdr date) 'org-air-face-date))
       (pill (eq org-air-date-style 'pill))
       (text (propertize (… (car date) …) 'face face)))
  (concat (if pill
              (org-air-view--svg-pillify text face :align org-air-date-pill-align)
            text)
          …))
```

Both the plain-text branch AND the pill branch are driven by the SAME
=face=.  Inside =org-air-view--svg-pillify= (org-air-view.el:3293):

#+begin_example
(fg (or (face-foreground face nil t) "gray"))
…
(svg-text svg label … :fill fg …)          ; the pill LABEL is drawn in FG
```

The capsule border is the neutral =org-air-pill-border= (a muted hairline;
=--item-date-text= passes NO =:border-color=), and there is no per-chip
fill — *the only colour on the pill is the label, and that colour is
=(face-foreground FACE)=* ("colour lives only in the LABEL via FACE",
org-air-view.el:3318 D-P1.LOOK).  Therefore:

- The pill reads "blue" *because* =--date-label='s scheduled/deadline arm
  handed =--svg-pillify= the SLOT face — there is NO hardcoded svg colour
  and NO svg-only code to change.
- Driving the day face into arms 3/4 recolours the pill LABEL to teal/rose
  automatically, in the SAME face-only edit that recolours the plain text.
  The fix reaches text AND svg with one change; no new pill parameter, no
  =:border-color=, no svg path edit.

*Which "blue"?*  In the palette (org-air-faces.el:81), =scheduled= inherits
=salient= = DeepPurple =#673AB7= (light) / *Nord9 =#81A1C1= (dark, a frost
BLUE)* — the dark-mode scheduled date is literally blue; the light-mode
one is a blue-leaning indigo.  =deadline= inherits =popout= = DeepOrange
=#E0631E= / Nord12 =#D08770= (orange/salmon).  A user who treats SCHEDULED
as their working "due/do date" sees the Nord9 blue; either way rule (A)
repaints a today/tomorrow such date to the teal/rose day face.

*GUI-confirm flag (dark-mode adjacency).*  =org-air-face-day-today='s dark
tier is Nord8 =#88C0D0= (frost cyan), ADJACENT to =scheduled='s dark Nord9
=#81A1C1= (frost blue).  So a *scheduled*-today in DARK mode moves from
Nord9→Nord8 — a real change, but a subtle one (cyan vs blue).  The
*deadline*-today (orange→teal) and *every light-mode* case are strong,
unambiguous standouts.  If GUI review finds the scheduled-today dark
delta too quiet, the follow-up is a one-line hex nudge on
=org-air-face-day-today='s dark tier (out of scope here — a face-hex tweak,
not a routing change); the ROUTING this round is correct regardless.

* Proposal

** D1 — route the day face into the DEADLINE and SCHEDULED arms (rule A)

In =org-air-view--date-label= (org-air-view.el:2076–2077), arms 3 and 4
gain the SAME =(or (--day-relative-face … now) 'org-air-face-SLOT)= wrap
that arm 6 already carries:

#+begin_example
     (deadline
      (cons (org-air-view--human-date deadline now)
            ;; R87: a deadline that falls today/tomorrow WINS its slot — it
            ;; reads teal/rose (day face) instead of the slot orange, so the
            ;; standout reaches the due-date column (text AND svg pill); a
            ;; deadline >=2 days out keeps `org-air-face-deadline'.
            (or (org-air-view--day-relative-face deadline now)
                'org-air-face-deadline)))
     (scheduled
      (cons (org-air-view--human-date scheduled now)
            ;; R87: a scheduled today/tomorrow WINS its slot too (consistency).
            (or (org-air-view--day-relative-face scheduled now)
                'org-air-face-scheduled)))
```

That is the WHOLE render change — two arms, mirroring R85's notes arm:

- The LABEL (=car=) is UNCHANGED — still exactly "Today" / "Tomorrow" /
  "Wed" / "17 Jul" (=--human-date= is untouched).  Byte layer intact.
- The FACE (=cdr=) becomes the day face for a today/tomorrow date, else
  the slot face exactly as before.
- The two =overdue= arms, =attention= and =stale= are UNTOUCHED (they never
  emit a relative today/tomorrow), so OVERDUE keeps =org-air-face-overdue=.
- The =notes= arm (R85) is UNCHANGED — it already does this.

No new scan slot, no cache-key element, no =org-air-view--cache-version=
bump — the deadline/scheduled timestamps and the delta are already
computed in this function; the day face is chosen from the SAME values
(R53).  A knob flip (light/dark, date-style) repaints, never rescans.
