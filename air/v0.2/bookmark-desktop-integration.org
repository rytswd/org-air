* Summary

R58 gave every org-air view an Emacs bookmark record (activities.el /
=bookmark-jump= compatible).  desktop.el integration
(=desktop-buffer-mode-handlers=) was ruled out of scope there, with the
note that "the bookmark records built here are the natural payload for a
future round".

* Proposal (sketch)

- Register =desktop-buffer-mode-handlers= entries for the org-air view
  modes; the handler rebuilds the view from the same record plist the R58
  bookmark maker produces (the restorer owns windows; org-air's reconciler
  adapts post-display, per the R58 rule).
- Non-goals carried over from R58: persisting rail/window layout,
  cross-machine portability.
