#+title: Dropped rows: faded re-pillify variant for tag/date pills (if the pills still shout)
#+state: draft
#+FILETAGS: :project:ui:polish:

* Summary

R48 greys dropped doc rows via the title + flex pad (dim + strike); the
pre-faced tag/date pill cells keep their own full-strength pill faces.  The
design ruled the title band dominant enough and explicitly noted a "faded
re-pillify variant" for the pills as out of scope, "noted for a future
round if the pills still shout".  Conditional polish — take it up only if
dogfooding confirms the pills still visually dominate dropped rows.

* Proposal (sketch)

- A faded pill variant (re-rasterised once per face, honouring the R47
  no-per-motion-rasterisation lock) applied to tag/date pills on dropped
  rows, wherever dropped rows are visible (expanded folds, collapse nil,
  filter-revealed).
