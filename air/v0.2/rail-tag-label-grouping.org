#+title: Rail/chip tag-label grouping for mixed #-prefixed datasets
#+state: draft
#+FILETAGS: :rail:tags:review:

* Summary
When a corpus mixes a plain tag (=nix=) AND a literal #-prefixed org tag (=#nix=), the R69 chip-label dedup makes both render as =#nix=, and any rollup/grouping that keys on the LABEL merges the two distinct tags into one bucket. Display-only dedup vs matching is correct (R69); the grouping key should be the underlying tag VALUE, not the rendered label.
