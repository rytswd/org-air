#+title: Review: dedup the completion chip by day, not exact epoch (spurious ×2 on mirrors)
#+state: ready
#+FILETAGS: :review:bug:

* Summary

The R63 review surface's stamp union dedups completion stamps by *exact
epoch*.  Mirrors DONE-logged minutes apart on the same day (the realistic
by-hand mirror flow) yield two distinct epochs, so one real completion shows
a spurious =×2= chip.  The R63 fixtures log mirrors at identical instants,
so no test sees it.  Flagged by the R63 Fable review as spec-faithful but
worth a future line.

* Proposal

- Dedup for the chip count at day granularity: count distinct *days* (or
  dedup epochs per key-day) when folding mirror stamps into one item's
  completion chip.
- Add the missing fixture shape: mirrors DONE-logged minutes apart on the
  same day ⇒ chip shows no multiplier; genuinely-repeated completions on
  different days keep their =×N=.
