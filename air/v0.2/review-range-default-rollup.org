#+title: Review: smarter default rollup per range (week-of for year)
#+state: draft
#+FILETAGS: :review:polish:

* Summary

R62's duration-range ladder keeps ='day= as the default Completed rollup at
every range: over a year that groups Completed into up to 365 day rows —
correct, verbose, and one =f= away from tag/directory/origin.  The R62
design explicitly left "a smarter default per range (e.g. week-of for year)"
out of scope as a future nicety.

* Proposal (sketch)

- Derive the default rollup from the active range scale: day for
  week/fortnight, week-of for quarter/year (month open to design).
- Explicit user choice (=f=) still wins and is remembered per the existing
  session behaviour; bookmark range carry composes unchanged.
