* Summary
Design deltas + exact face specs for the visual half of round 4
(=org-air-modern.org=): T1 colour & Org-aware styling, T3 calendar
spacing + multi-date legend, T7 boxed frame (buffer-box), and the visual
half of T4 (return-to-dashboard display contract).  impl2 takes the
interaction bugs (T2/T4-logic/T5/T6).

*Measurability contract (read first).*  The byte gate asserts *buffer
text*.  Three kinds of change here:
- *Face-only* (text byte-identical) \u2014 safe, fixtures unaffected.  Applied
  now to =org-air-faces.el=: T1 keyword/priority colour, T3 mark faces,
  T7 frame-border face.
- *Buffer-text* (glyph/spacing) \u2014 needs a fixture regen; *flagged*, not
  silently applied: T3a column spacing, T3b mark glyphs, T1 chip padding
  if any.
- *Window chrome / display props* (margins, header-line, mode-line,
  line-prefix, wrap-prefix, =:box=) \u2014 *NOT visible to =buffer-substring=*;
  the gate cannot assert them.  Every such prop is flagged =[non-byte]=.

* T1 — Colour & Org-aware styling
*Symptom (screenshot 4):* TODO keywords, =[#A]= cookies and =#tags= all
render monochrome grey; the accent palette is dead on screen.  User: "a
bit more coloured to give modern look and feel" (org-modern-ish, NOT
full Org parity).

** T1a — Coloured TODO keywords (keyword \u2192 face map)
Map keywords to faces (org-modern style); render the keyword in its
face.  Default map =org-air-todo-keyword-faces= (defcustom):
| Keyword(s)            | Face                         | Colour role        |
|-----------------------+------------------------------+--------------------|
| TODO                  | =org-air-face-todo=            | salient (active)   |
| NEXT / STARTED        | =org-air-face-todo-next=       | popout (hot)       |
| WAIT / HOLD / BLOCKED | =org-air-face-todo-wait=       | faded-warm         |
| DONE                  | =org-air-face-done=            | faded green/quiet  |
| CANCELLED / KILL      | =org-air-face-done=            | faded, strike      |
Unknown keywords fall back to =org-air-face-todo=.  Faces (added to
=org-air-faces.el=):
#+begin_src elisp
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-todo-next
  '((t :inherit org-air-face-popout :weight bold))
  "Active 'hot' keyword (NEXT/STARTED).")
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-todo-wait
  '((((class color)(min-colors 256)(background light)) (:foreground "#B26A00" :weight bold))
    (((class color)(min-colors 256)(background dark))  (:foreground "#D8A657" :weight bold))
    (t (:inherit org-air-face-faded :weight bold)))
  "Waiting/blocked keyword \u2014 a muted amber, distinct from active.")
#+end_src
(=org-air-face-todo= stays salient-bold; for a punchier active colour the
user can remap it.)  *Render note:* the keyword text is unchanged
(\"TODO\"), so this is *face-only* \u2014 fixtures hold.

** T1b — Boxed priority cookies (=[#A]= = a coloured pill)
Priority cookies become small coloured boxes, per level.  =:box= is a
*GUI display attribute* (TTY ignores it gracefully \u2014 the cookie stays
coloured text), so this is *face-only for the byte gate* (text =[#A]=
unchanged).  Per-level faces:
#+begin_src elisp
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-priority-a
  '((((class color)(min-colors 256)(background light))
     (:foreground "#FFFFFF" :background "#C62828" :weight bold :box (:line-width (1 . -1) :color "#C62828")))
    (((class color)(min-colors 256)(background dark))
     (:foreground "#2E3440" :background "#EBCB8B" :weight bold :box (:line-width (1 . -1) :color "#EBCB8B")))
    (t (:inherit org-air-face-critical :weight bold)))
  "[#A] cookie: a filled critical pill.")
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-priority-b
  '((t :inherit org-air-face-popout :weight bold :box (:line-width (1 . -1))))
  "[#B] cookie: popout, lightly boxed.")
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-priority-c
  '((t :inherit org-air-face-faded :box (:line-width (1 . -1))))
  "[#C] cookie: faded, lightly boxed.")
#+end_src
=org-air-face-priority= remains the fallback.  Map by char in the render
(=?A \u2192 -a=, =?B \u2192 -b=, else =-c=).  [non-byte for the =:box= pixels; the
text and the foreground colour are testable.]

** T1c — Tag chips actually use the accent palette
The accent faces (=org-air-face-tag-accent-1..6=) and
=org-air-faces-tag-face= already exist but the screen shows grey \u2014 the
renderer must call =(org-air-faces-tag-face tag)= per chip (it may be
applying a flat =org-air-face-tag=).  *Fix in render* (impl): each =#tag=
gets its deterministic accent face.  *Face tune (this round):* the
dark-mode accents share one background (=#3B4252=), so chips read as
\"grey with faint colour\" against the dark dashboard; raise dark-mode
*foreground* vividness and drop the shared bg to transparent so the hue
carries.  Revised dark tier per accent = a saturated foreground, no
background (light mode keeps the tinted pill):

;; 4 orange #D08770 · 5 teal #8FBCBB · 6 pink #BF616A   (no dark bg fill)
#+end_src
*Render note:* tag text unchanged \u2192 *face-only*, fixtures hold; only the
chip colours change.

* T3 — Calendar spacing + multi-date legend
** T3a — One extra space between day columns (RESPONSIVE)  [BYTE → regen]
The user asked for breathing room: a 4-col day cell (%2d + marker +
space) instead of the packed 3-col cell.  *Conflict (impl2 ozysznst):*
the narrow rail tier holds only ~26 cols of calendar at 95–119-col
windows, so a 28-wide (4-col) calendar truncates Sunday (…Sa  S…).

*RULING — responsive calendar (option B).*  Day spacing depends on the
rail tier; do NOT widen the rail or shift the breakpoint:
| Window width | Rail content | Calendar | Day cell             | Row |
|--------------+--------------+----------+----------------------+-----|
| 95–119       | 28 (narrow)  | compact  | %2d + marker         |  21 |
| ≥ 120        | 32 / 42      | spaced   | %2d + marker + space | ~28 |
Rule: use the 4-col *spaced* calendar when rail content width ≥ 30 (the
mid/wide tiers, windows ≥ 120); else the 3-col *compact* calendar.  Both
fit their tier (compact 21 ≤ 26; spaced ≤ ~28 ≤ 30) — no Sunday
truncation, no breakpoint change.  The weekday header pads to the active
cell width.  The user's wide GUI windows (the screenshots) get the
requested spacing; narrow laptops stay compact rather than clip.  Expose
=org-air-calendar-day-spacing= (default 'auto; 0/1 to force).  Changes
the calendar bytes in the ≥120 tiers → *fixture regen*; the pane-divider
column is unaffected (the rail content width absorbs it — confirm at
regen).
** T3b — Distinguish CREATED / SCHEDULED / DEADLINE  [BYTE \u2192 regen]
Today every dated day uses one =\u25cf= in =org-air-face-calendar-event=.
Split by the *strongest* timestamp on that day, by colour (and a distinct
glyph for colour-blind / TTY safety):
| Kind      | Mark | Face                            | Colour   |
|-----------+------+---------------------------------+----------|
| deadline  | =\u25c6=    | =org-air-face-calendar-deadline=  | popout   |
| scheduled | =\u25cf=    | =org-air-face-calendar-scheduled= | salient  |
| created   | =\u00b7=    | =org-air-face-calendar-created=   | faded    |
| today     | =\u25a0=    | =org-air-face-calendar-today=     | popout+ul |
Precedence on a day with several: deadline > scheduled > created.
Faces (added now, face-only until the render uses them):
#+begin_src elisp
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-calendar-scheduled
  '((t :inherit org-air-face-salient :weight bold)) "Calendar day with a scheduled item.")
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-calendar-deadline
  '((t :inherit org-air-face-popout :weight bold)) "Calendar day with a deadline.")
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-calendar-created
  '((t :inherit org-air-face-faded)) "Calendar day with only a created/activity stamp.")
#+end_src
*3-way legend* (replaces =\u25cf has items \u00b7 \u25a0 today=), markers shown in their
real faces so the legend doubles as a key (cf. S9):
: \u25c6 deadline \u00b7 \u25cf scheduled \u00b7 \u00b7 created \u00b7 \u25a0 today
Legend text *changes* \u2192 *flag for regen*; uses =org-air-face-calendar-
legend= (S9) for the words, mark faces for the samples.

* T7 — Boxed frame (buffer-box)
*Goal:* replace the lone =\u2502= rail divider with a proper framed dashboard,
after rougier/buffer-box: a *one-character border on all sides* drawn in
*window chrome*, not buffer text.

** Mechanism (per buffer-box) \u2014 all [non-byte]
- *Left/right borders:* =left-margin-width = right-margin-width = 1=; put
  the border glyph in =line-prefix= and =wrap-prefix= (left =\u2502=) and a
  right-edge overlay / =:align-to right= space carrying =\u2502= for the right.
- *Top border:* =header-line-format= styled as =\u250c\u2500\u2500\u2026\u2500\u2500\u2510=.
- *Bottom border:* =mode-line-format= (a minimal line) styled as
  =\u2514\u2500\u2500\u2026\u2500\u2500\u2518=.
- *Border face:* new =org-air-face-frame-border= (faded foreground; the
  buffer-box look is a quiet 1px rule).
- *TTY fallback:* margins still work; border glyphs degrade =\u2502\u2192|=,
  =\u2500\u2192-=, corners =\u2192+= (existing glyph table); if margins are unavailable
  the frame is simply dropped (whitespace) \u2014 no content lost.
#+begin_src elisp
(custom-declare-face 'org-air-face-frame-border
  '((t :inherit org-air-face-faded))
  "Face for the buffer-box outer frame (margins/header-line/mode-line).")
#+end_src

** What stays in-buffer (byte-tested)
- The header band (line 1: =org-air \u2026 status=), rules, item pane, rail,
  the *internal* rail divider =\u2502=, and the footer remain buffer text and
  keep their byte fixtures.  The outer frame wraps *around* them.
- So the composed look = a thin outer box (chrome) containing the
  existing two-pane content with its internal =\u2502= sidebar split \u2014 an
  \"app with a sidebar,\" which is exactly what the lone divider was
  reaching for.  The divider no longer looks lonely because it is now
  one line of a frame bounded top/bottom/left/right.

** Decision: outer-box + retained internal divider (option A)
Chosen over (B) boxing only the rail, or (C) boxing both panes in-buffer,
because A: (1) applies buffer-box directly, (2) preserves byte-testability
of ALL content, (3) confines the unmeasurable parts to clearly-flagged
chrome.  (B)/(C) noted as alternatives if a discrete boxed rail widget is
later preferred; those would put =\u250c\u2500\u2510= in buffer text (byte-tested) at the
cost of content columns.

** T7 vs S1 (ruling)
S1 removed a *duplicate banner*: the title+status was rendered twice
(header-line AND buffer line 1), and the header-line copy clipped
off-screen.  *S1's intent is "no duplicate banner in the header-line,"
NOT "header-line-format must be nil."*  The single source of the
title+status is buffer line 1 (measurable).  A header-line used purely
as the buffer-box *top border* (=┌─…─┐= frame chrome, carrying no
duplicate title/status text) is therefore *allowed and faithful to S1*.
Test should relax the invariant from =header-line-format= is nil to:
*the title+status string appears exactly once in the buffer (line 1),
and the header-line contains no banner/status duplicate* — a frame border
is fine.  Confirmed faithful to the S1 design.

** Header/mode-line content
Keep the *measurable* title+status on buffer line 1 (S1/S7).  The
header-line top border may ALSO carry a faint repeat of =org-air= at its
left (buffer-box puts the filename in the header) \u2014 optional, non-byte.
The bottom mode-line border can host the key legend (moving it out of the
buffer), OR keep the footer in-buffer and let the mode-line be a bare
=\u2514\u2500\u2518=.  *Recommendation:* keep the footer legend in-buffer (tested);
mode-line = bare bottom border.

* T4 (visual half) — return-to-dashboard display contract
The interaction logic is impl2's; the *display contract* is here.
- =org-air-visit-display= (existing) gains a documented set:
  ='other-window= (default), ='same= (reuse the dashboard window),
  ='side= (a reusable side window the dashboard owns), ='dashboard=
  (render the item *inside* a peek region of the dashboard buffer).
- *Return contract:* binding =q= in the visited Org buffer (when reached
  via org-air) runs =org-air-return= \u2014 restores the *exact* window
  configuration captured at visit time (store it in a buffer-local /
  ring), landing point back on the originating item row.  Never
  =kill-buffer= the user's Org file; never leave a broken split.
- =org-air-visit-item-stay= / =SPC= peek keeps focus on the dashboard
  (already specced); =RET= follows =org-air-visit-display=.
- *Display-action defcustom* =org-air-display-action= for =org-air= itself
  (round-3 D4): full-width reuse so the rail never lands off-screen.
- Faces: none new; the peek region (='dashboard= strategy) dims the
  dashboard with =org-air-face-faded= and shows the item in normal faces,
  reusing the process-inbox dimming pattern.
