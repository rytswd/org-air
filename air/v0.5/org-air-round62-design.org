* Item 2 — the duration-range ladder

** The ladder

#+begin_example
week ←→ fortnight ←→ month ←→ quarter ←→ year
        (2 ISO weeks)         (3 months)  (calendar)
#+end_example

=org-air-review--period-kind='s domain widens from
='week=/='month= to the five symbols.  One new knob,
=org-air-review-ranges= (defcustom, org-air-review.el, default the
full five in ladder order): the ranges =+=/=-=/=m= walk.  Validation
at use, never trusted raw: unknown symbols are dropped, an empty
result degrades to ='(week month)=, and a current kind not in the
trimmed ladder participates at its natural rank so the user is never
trapped on an unreachable rung.  Render-time only — the knob, like
the kind and anchor, is NOT a cache-key element (the R61-2/R57
discipline: only scan-shaping knobs join the key).

** Period math (each new range, oracle-verified)

All three new branches land in =org-air-review--period-bounds= beside
the existing two, returning the same half-open integer
=[START, END)= built by =org-air-review--date-epoch= on LOCAL
calendar dates — DST-exactness is inherited by construction, and
R61's =--clip=, =--day-bounds=, the section predicates, the rollup
fold, the suspect rule and the calendar MARKS builder consume the
bounds UNCHANGED.  No other engine function is touched.

- /fortnight/ — FIXED-PHASE Monday-anchored 14-day blocks:
  =start-abs = A − ((A − PHASE) mod 14)= over =calendar-absolute=
  day numbers, PHASE = the absolute day of Monday 1970-01-05 (a
  =defconst=; =calendar-day-of-week= verified 1).  Every fortnight
  is exactly two consecutive ISO weeks (both are Monday-anchored
  absolute-day blocks).  RULED OUT — the "obvious" ISO-year-local
  odd/even week pairing: a 53-week ISO year (2026 IS one — W53
  runs Dec 28 … Jan 3) leaves W53 unpaired, so year-local pairing
  either overlaps the next year's first fortnight or produces a
  7-day "fortnight" — both break the partition law (every instant
  in exactly ONE period) that =<=/=>= adjacency and clip
  complementarity are built on.  The fixed phase has no year
  seam: measured at the W53/2026 edge, [2026-12-21, 2027-01-04)
  then [2027-01-04, 2027-01-18), both exactly 14 days.  Which of
  a week pair leads is phase-determined, not year-determined —
  documented in the docstring, asserted in T6.
- /quarter/ — calendar quarters Jan–Mar / Apr–Jun / Jul–Sep /
  Oct–Dec: start month =1 + 3⌊(m−1)/3⌋=, 1st 00:00 → the +3
  month's 1st (December-style year rollover for Q4).
- /year/ — Jan 1 00:00 → next Jan 1.

The verification battery (=/tmp/org-air-r62/range-math.el=, becomes
T6's oracle): the ADJACENCY LAW — =bounds(kind, END)= starts at END,
=bounds(kind, START−1)= ends at START, and the anchor lies inside its
own bounds — over all 5 kinds × 9 edge dates (ISO year edges
2026-01-01/2027-01-01, quarter edges Mar 31/Apr 1, Dec 31, the
W53 Monday Dec 28, leap Feb 29 2028, DST transition Sundays) × 2
timezones (the local DST-less one and =America/New_York=): ALL PASS.
Clip complementarity across each NEW boundary: a 90-minute interval
straddling a fortnight/quarter/year edge splits 60 + 30 exactly.

** Navigation is already generic — and what changes

=<= / =>= / =.= need ZERO changes: =period-prev= anchors to
=bounds(kind, START−1).start=, =period-next= to the half-open END,
=.= clears the anchor — all pure functions of the active kind's
bounds, so they step by ONE unit of the active range for free (the
adjacency law above IS the proof; T7 asserts it per kind at the
command level).

New commands (R62-3):

- =org-air-review-range-widen= (=+=, alias == for the unshifted
  key) / =org-air-review-range-narrow= (=-=): one rung along the
  effective ladder, CLAMPED at the ends with a bounded message
  ("widest range" / "narrowest range") — no wrap, so repeated =+=
  parks safely at year.
- =org-air-review-cycle-range= (=m=): cycles the effective ladder
  with wrap-around.  This GENERALISES =org-air-review-toggle-kind=
  (kept as an obsolete alias): with the ladder knob trimmed to
  ='(week month)= the cycle IS the old toggle.  DELIBERATE behaviour
  change at the default ladder — =m= from week now lands fortnight,
  not month — because two overlapping span mechanisms (a 2-state
  toggle beside a 5-rung ladder) would leave =m= undefined on the
  three new rungs; one ladder, three verbs (=+= / =-= directional,
  =m= rotary).  R61 T14's command-level =m= assertions are UPDATED
  to the ladder (its pure-engine oracle asserts stand untouched);

All three set the kind ONLY — the anchor is preserved, and the shown
period is the new range's period CONTAINING the anchor (the R61

|-----------+--------------------+-----------------------------------------|

arithmetic on the first week's number, so W52/W53 pairings label

Sections and rollups fold identically at every range (they are
parameterised on =[P0, P1)= only).  Noted, not changed: the default
